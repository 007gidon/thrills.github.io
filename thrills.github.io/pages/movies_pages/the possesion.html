The Possession

"The Possession" (2012) is a supernatural horror film directed by Ole Bornedal. The story revolves around a young girl named Em who becomes possessed by a malevolent entity after purchasing an antique wooden box at a yard sale. As Em's behavior becomes increasingly erratic and violent, her parents, Clyde and Stephanie, must confront the terrifying reality of demonic possession and seek help to save their daughter. The film explores themes of family dynamics, faith, and the struggle against evil forces beyond comprehension. Clyde and Stephanie enlist the help of experts in Jewish mysticism to perform an exorcism and rid Em of the malevolent spirit. Set against the backdrop of a suburban American neighborhood, "The Possession" delivers chilling scares and suspenseful sequences as the family grapples with the sinister influence of the ancient box and the supernatural entity it contains. With its atmospheric tension and creepy imagery, the film keeps viewers on the edge of their seats until the climactic showdown between good and evil. Featuring standout performances from Jeffrey Dean Morgan and Kyra Sedgwick as the desperate parents and Natasha Calis as the possessed girl, "The Possession" offers a haunting and chilling cinematic experience for fans of supernatural horror.

"The Possession" embodies a unique blend of two distinct horror subgenres:

  • Supernatural Horror:

    This subgenre focuses on supernatural elements such as ghosts, demons, or otherworldly forces. In "The Possession," the main plot revolves around a young girl who becomes possessed by a malevolent entity after acquiring an antique box, leading to terrifying supernatural occurrences.

  • Demonic Possession:

    Within the realm of supernatural horror, demonic possession specifically involves a person being controlled or influenced by a demonic entity. In "The Possession," the central conflict arises when the young girl falls victim to demonic possession after opening the cursed box, leading to increasingly frightening and dangerous behavior.
