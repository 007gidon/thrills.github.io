"Possession" is a 1981 French-German psychological horror film directed by Andrzej Żuławski. The film follows a couple, Anna and Mark, who are going through a bitter divorce. Their relationship becomes increasingly twisted and violent as Anna's behavior becomes more erratic. Mark becomes suspicious and starts to investigate, uncovering a dark and disturbing secret. Thematically, "Possession" delves into obsession, madness, and the breakdown of a relationship, exploring the inner turmoil of its characters amidst surreal and intense imagery. Starring Isabelle Adjani and Sam Neill, the film received mixed reviews upon release but has since gained a cult following for its bold storytelling and artistic vision, influencing later filmmakers with its unsettling atmosphere and provocative narrative.

    "Planet Terror" falls into the zombie horror subgenre, featuring an outbreak of a deadly virus that turns people into flesh-eating zombies. The film showcases graphic violence, gore, and intense action as the characters battle against the zombie hordes.

  • French Extreme:

    The film pays homage to exploitation cinema of the 1970s, with its gritty visuals, over-the-top violence, and stylized presentation. "Planet Terror" embraces the grindhouse aesthetic, including elements like film scratches, missing reels, and intentionally cheesy dialogue.

  • Action Horror:

    "Planet Terror" combines elements of action and horror, featuring intense and thrilling action sequences alongside its horror elements. The film delivers fast-paced and adrenaline-fueled scenes of combat and survival as the characters fight against the zombie threat.
