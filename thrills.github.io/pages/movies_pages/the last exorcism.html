The Last Exorcism

"The Last Exorcism" is a 2010 American found footage supernatural horror film directed by Daniel Stamm. The film stars Patrick Fabian, Ashley Bell, Iris Bahr, Caleb Landry Jones, and Louis Herthum. It follows the story of a disillusioned evangelical minister named Cotton Marcus, who decides to participate in a documentary chronicling his last exorcism while also exposing the fraudulent nature of his ministry. When Cotton receives a letter from a farmer seeking help in driving out the devil from his daughter, he agrees to perform one final exorcism for the cameras. Upon arriving at the remote farm, Cotton meets the farmer's afflicted daughter, Nell, and begins the exorcism ritual. However, as the exorcism progresses, Cotton and the documentary crew uncover disturbing truths about Nell's possession and the sinister forces at work. "The Last Exorcism" received positive reviews from critics for its unique approach to the found footage genre and its effective blend of supernatural horror and psychological thrills. The film was also a box office success, grossing over $67 million worldwide against a modest budget of $1.8 million. A sequel titled "The Last Exorcism Part II" was released in 2013, though it did not feature the found footage format of its predecessor.

"The Last Exorcism" embodies a unique blend of two distinct horror subgenres:

  • Found Footage Horror:

    The film is presented as a documentary-style found footage movie, following a disillusioned minister named Cotton Marcus as he performs his final exorcism. The found footage format adds a sense of realism and immediacy to the horror, as viewers experience the events through the perspective of the documentary crew.

  • Possession Horror:

    A central aspect of the film involves the exorcism of a young girl named Nell, who is believed to be possessed by a demonic entity. The possession theme drives much of the horror, as Cotton Marcus and the documentary crew confront the sinister forces at work and the terrifying consequences of Nell's possession.
