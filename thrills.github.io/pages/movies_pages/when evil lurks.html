when evil lurks

"When Evil Lurks" (Spanish: "Cuando acecha la maldad") is a 2023 supernatural folk psychological horror film directed by Demián Rugna. The story follows brothers Pedro and Jaime, who discover a possessed man's body in the woods and encounter a family hiding from a demonic presence. As they seek help, chaos ensues, with possessions spreading rapidly and leading to devastating consequences. Pedro and Jaime's attempts to seek assistance from the authorities and a local landowner fail, resulting in the rapid escalation of demonic possession in their town. The brothers face personal tragedies as they try to protect their loved ones from the spreading evil. With possessed individuals causing chaos and death, Pedro and Jaime must confront the demonic entity responsible for the horrors unfolding around them. As the situation spirals out of control, the brothers face harrowing challenges, including the loss of their family members to possession and violence. Despite their efforts to escape the town and find safety, they are ultimately forced to confront the demonic presence head-on in a desperate bid to survive. The film explores themes of family, survival, and the insidious nature of evil, offering a chilling and suspenseful journey into the darkness lurking beneath the surface of ordinary life.

"when evil lurks" embodies a unique blend of three distinct horror subgenres:

  • Supernatural Horror:

    The film revolves around a supernatural threat, specifically the possession of individuals by demons, which drives the central conflict and horror elements.

  • Psychological Horror:

    The possession aspect adds psychological tension, as characters grapple with the fear and uncertainty of not knowing who can be trusted and the sinister motivations behind the demonic influence.

  • Splatter Horror:

    The film contains extreme and graphic violence, including scenes of gore and bodily mutilation, contributing to the splatter horror subgenre's visceral and shocking nature.
