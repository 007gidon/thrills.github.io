Cherry Falls

"Cherry Falls," released in 1999, is a compelling addition to the slasher genre, directed by Geoffrey Wright and boasting a talented cast including Brittany Murphy, Jay Mohr, and Michael Biehn. Set in a quaint Virginia town, the film unravels a chilling tale of terror as a serial killer targets teenage virgins, sending shockwaves of fear throughout the community. As the body count rises, the town's residents are plunged into a state of panic and suspicion, grappling with the sinister mystery lurking in their midst. Despite its initial struggles with the MPAA and a delayed theatrical release in the United States, "Cherry Falls" made its mark on the international film circuit with premieres at prestigious events like Marché du Film in France and screenings at various film festivals. Finally finding a home with USA Films, the movie captivated audiences with its gripping narrative and innovative approach to the slasher genre. With its intriguing premise and standout performances, "Cherry Falls" offers more than just thrills and chills—it delves into themes of innocence, sexuality, and the dark underbelly of suburban life, adding depth and complexity to its terrifying tale. As viewers are drawn into the web of intrigue and suspense, they're left pondering the true identity of the killer and the secrets hidden within the seemingly idyllic town of Cherry Falls.
