The Possession of Hannah Grace

"The Possession of Hannah Grace," also known as "Cadaver" in some countries, is a 2018 American supernatural horror thriller film directed by Diederik van Rooijen and written by Brian Sieve. Starring Shay Mitchell, Kirby Johnson, Stana Katic, Grey Damon, and Nick Thune, the film centers around Megan Reed, a former policewoman who takes a job as an overnight intake assistant at a morgue. As Megan begins her new job, she encounters the supernatural when the body of a young woman named Hannah Grace arrives. Haunted by disturbing visions and strange occurrences, Megan becomes increasingly convinced that there is something malevolent lurking within the morgue. As she investigates further, Megan finds herself entangled in a terrifying battle against a powerful demonic entity. Released in the United States on November 30, 2018, by Sony Pictures Releasing under the Screen Gems label, "The Possession of Hannah Grace" received generally negative reviews from critics. However, despite the critical reception, the film proved to be commercially successful, grossing $43 million worldwide.

"The Possession of Hannah Grace" embodies a unique blend of two distinct horror subgenres:

  • Possession Horror:

    As indicated by the title, the film revolves around the possession of Hannah Grace's body by a malevolent entity. The possession aspect drives much of the horror in the film, as Hannah's body becomes a vessel for demonic forces, leading to terrifying and gruesome encounters.

  • Supernatural Thriller:

    The film contains supernatural elements, including the presence of a demonic entity and paranormal occurrences surrounding Hannah Grace's body. These supernatural elements contribute to the tension and suspense throughout the film, as characters grapple with the terrifying reality of facing supernatural forces.
