As Above, So Below

"As Above, So Below" offers a unique blend of horror and adventure, taking audiences on a harrowing journey through the eerie depths of the Catacombs of Paris. The film's found footage style adds a layer of authenticity, immersing viewers in the visceral experience of exploration and discovery alongside the characters. As the documentary crew delves deeper into the catacombs, they encounter chilling manifestations and unearth ancient secrets, drawing parallels to Dante Alighieri's Divine Comedy and its depiction of the nine circles of Hell. Led by a talented cast including Perdita Weeks, Ben Feldman, and Edwin Hodge, the film delves into themes of obsession, redemption, and the human psyche's darkest depths. Director John Erick Dowdle masterfully crafts a suspenseful and atmospheric narrative, blending elements of horror, mystery, and psychological thriller to keep audiences on the edge of their seats. Produced by Legendary Pictures and distributed by Universal Pictures, "As Above, So Below" marks the beginning of a successful partnership between the two studios. Despite mixed reviews from critics, the film's box office success surpassed expectations, cementing its status as a cult favorite among horror enthusiasts. With its chilling premise, compelling characters, and immersive storytelling, "As Above, So Below" continues to captivate audiences with its exploration of the unknown and the supernatural.
