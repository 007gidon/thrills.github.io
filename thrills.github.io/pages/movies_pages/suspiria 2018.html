Suspiria

"Suspiria" (2018) is a supernatural horror film directed by Luca Guadagnino, a remake of Dario Argento's 1977 classic. The story follows an American dancer, played by Dakota Johnson, who joins a prestigious academy in Berlin led by a coven of witches. Tilda Swinton takes on multiple roles, including the lead choreographer and the head of the coven. The screenplay by David Kajganich explores themes of generational guilt, motherhood, and evil against the backdrop of the "German Autumn" of 1977, a turbulent time in the country's history. Unlike the original film's vibrant colors, Guadagnino opts for a more muted and bleak visual style. The film includes stylized dance sequences choreographed by Damien Jalet, adding to its eerie atmosphere. Filming took place in Italy and Berlin, with a score composed by Thom Yorke of Radiohead fame. "Suspiria" premiered at the 75th Venice International Film Festival and had a limited release in October 2018, followed by a wider release in November. Despite polarized reviews, it garnered attention for its ambitious themes and visually striking presentation.
