Cocaine Bear

"Cocaine Bear" is a 2023 American comedy horror film directed by Elizabeth Banks and written by Jimmy Warden. Loosely inspired by the true story of a bear that ingested cocaine, the film stars Keri Russell, O'Shea Jackson Jr., Alden Ehrenreich, and Ray Liotta. Set in 1985, the movie follows the chaotic events that unfold after a drug smuggler drops cocaine from his plane, leading to a bear ingesting the drugs and going on a rampage in the Chattahoochee National Forest. As various characters get entangled in the bear's path, including hikers, drug dealers, and law enforcement, hilarity and chaos ensue. Despite the film's violent and absurd premise, it knows exactly what it is and delivers on its promise of over-the-top entertainment. With impressive CGI effects bringing the bear to life and a blend of humor and gore, "Cocaine Bear" is a wild ride from start to finish. While it briefly veers into sentimentality, the film ultimately stands out as a fun and memorable cinematic experience.
