The Silence

"The Silence" presents a chilling tale set in a world besieged by deadly creatures that hunt by sound. Directed by John R. Leonetti and featuring a talented cast including Kiernan Shipka, Stanley Tucci, Miranda Otto, and John Corbett, the film unfolds a gripping narrative adapted from Tim Lebbon's 2015 horror novel. In this post-apocalyptic landscape, Shipka's character, a deaf teenager, navigates the treacherous terrain with her family as they seek refuge from the relentless creatures. However, their sanctuary is threatened by a sinister cult that seeks to exploit Shipka's unique condition for their own nefarious purposes. As tension mounts and danger lurks at every turn, the characters must confront not only the external threats of the creatures but also the internal conflicts and moral dilemmas that arise in the face of adversity. With survival hanging in the balance, alliances are tested, secrets are revealed, and sacrifices must be made to ensure the family's survival in this harrowing new world. Released by Netflix on April 10, 2019, "The Silence" captivated audiences with its suspenseful storytelling, atmospheric cinematography, and standout performances. While also enjoying a limited theatrical release in seven countries, the film garnered praise for its tense atmosphere and thought-provoking themes, making it a noteworthy addition to the horror genre.

"The Silence" embodies a unique blend of two distinct horror subgenres:

  • Creature Feature Horror:

    "The Silence" falls into the creature feature subgenre, focusing on the deadly creatures known as Vesps that hunt humans based on sound. The film creates tension and suspense through the relentless pursuit of the creatures and the characters' desperate attempts to avoid making noise.

  • Post-Apocalyptic Horror::

    "The Silence" is set in a post-apocalyptic world where society has collapsed due to the invasion of the Vesps. The film explores the aftermath of the invasion and the challenges faced by the remaining survivors as they adapt to a new way of life in silence.
