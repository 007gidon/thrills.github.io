Pearl

Pearl (subtitled An X-traordinary Origin Story) is a 2022 American horror film directed by Ti West, co-written by West and Mia Goth, who reprises her role as the title character, and featuring David Corenswet, Tandi Wright, Matthew Sunderland and Emma Jenkins-Purro in supporting roles. A prequel to X (2022) and the second installment in the X film series, it serves as an origin story for the title villain, whose fervent aspiration to become a movie star led her to committing violent acts on her family's Texas homestead in 1918. West began co-writing a prequel script stemming from his collaboration with Goth while filming X. Motivated by the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on cinema, filming started in New Zealand immediately after the first film, using X sets and the Avatar: The Way of Water crew and taking pandemic safety precautions. Pearl drew inspiration from the works of Douglas Sirk, Technicolor films like The Wizard of Oz (1939) and Mary Poppins (1964), and Disney films. It had its world premiere at the 79th Venice International Film Festival on September 3, 2022, and was released in theaters in the United States on September 16, 2022, by A24. The film grossed over $10 million and received generally positive reviews from critics, with Goth's performance receiving wide praise.

  • Slasher Horror:

    "Pearl" incorporates elements of the slasher subgenre, featuring moments of intense violence and horror reminiscent of classic slasher tropes. The film presents unsettling scenes of Pearl's violent outbursts and disturbing actions, echoing the trademark traits of slasher villains. These elements contribute to the film's eerie atmosphere and suspense, adding a layer of tension to Pearl's psychological unraveling.

  • Psychological Horror:

    Beyond its slasher elements, "Pearl" delves into the psychological turmoil of its titular character. The film primarily focuses on Pearl's desperate quest for recognition and her psychological unraveling. Through nuanced character exploration and thematic depth, "Pearl" navigates themes of isolation, obsession, and the corrosive nature of fame. As Pearl's psyche deteriorates, the audience is drawn into a gripping psychological thriller that explores the depths of human obsession and desperation.
