Tusk

"Tusk" is a 2014 horror-comedy film written and directed by Kevin Smith. The film stars Justin Long, Michael Parks, Haley Joel Osment, and Genesis Rodriguez. "Tusk" follows the story of a podcaster who is captured and transformed into a walrus by a deranged seafarer. In "Tusk," Wallace Bryton, a podcaster, travels to Canada to interview an eccentric seafarer named Howard Howe. However, Wallace soon discovers that Howard has sinister intentions and becomes his captive. Howard reveals his plan to surgically transform Wallace into a walrus as a twisted form of companionship, inspired by his own encounters with the creature. The film combines elements of body horror, dark comedy, and psychological thriller as Wallace undergoes his horrifying transformation and his friends, including his girlfriend and podcast co-host, attempt to rescue him. The grotesque and surreal premise of "Tusk" lends itself to both comedic and disturbing moments, blurring the lines between laughter and revulsion. While "Tusk" received mixed reviews from critics upon its release, it has gained a cult following for its unique premise and memorable performances, particularly Michael Parks as the enigmatic Howard Howe. The film's unconventional narrative and willingness to push boundaries make it a standout entry in the horror-comedy genre, offering audiences an unforgettable and bizarre cinematic experience.

"Tusk" embodies a unique blend of two distinct horror subgenres:

  • Body Horror:

    "Tusk" heavily relies on body horror, particularly in its depiction of the protagonist's transformation into a grotesque walrus-like creature. The film features graphic and disturbing scenes of surgical mutilation and physical transformation, evoking feelings of revulsion and terror.

  • Mad Scientist Horror:

    The antagonist of "Tusk" is a deranged surgeon named Howard Howe, who kidnaps unsuspecting victims and performs horrific experiments on them. The film explores themes of scientific hubris and the dangers of unchecked ambition, typical of the mad scientist subgenre.
