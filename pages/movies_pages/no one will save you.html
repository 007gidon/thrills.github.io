No One Will Save You

"No One Will Save You" is a 2023 American science fiction horror film written, directed, and produced by Brian Duffield. The film stars Kaitlyn Dever as Brynn, a young woman living alone in a forest on the outskirts of a small town, mourning the loss of her best friend and mother. Brynn's solitary existence is disrupted when her home is invaded by an unearthly creature, forcing her to confront her past and fight for survival. Notably, the film contains only five words of dialogue throughout its entire runtime. Released on September 19, 2023, the film received attention for its creative use of sound and tension-building techniques. While praised for its initial suspenseful sequences, some critics felt that the film faltered in maintaining its momentum and lacked narrative context. Despite Kaitlyn Dever's strong performance, the film was criticized for its repetitive alien sequences and the absence of a grounded center. Overall, "No One Will Save You" was seen as a promising concept that fell short in its execution.

"No One Will Save You" embodies a unique blend of two distinct horror subgenres:

  • Science Fiction Horror:

    The film prominently features elements of science fiction, particularly with the presence of an unearthly creature invading the protagonist's home, creating a sense of horror and suspense.

  • Survival Horror:

    As the protagonist, Brynn, confronts the otherworldly threat and fights for her survival, the film falls into the survival horror subgenre, characterized by tense and thrilling sequences of evasion and confrontation.
