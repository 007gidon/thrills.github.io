Hell House LLC

Hell House LLC is a 2015 American found-footage horror film written and directed by Stephen Cognetti and produced by Joe Bandelli. The film, shot as a documentary, follows a group of Halloween haunted house creators as they prepare for the 2009 opening of their popular haunted attraction, Hell House. Tragedy strikes on opening night when an unknown "malfunction" causes the death of 15 tour-goers and staff. The film reveals the lead-up to the tragedy and what really went wrong that night, the details of which have remained a mystery to the public. The film was released on a number of video on-demand platforms, including Amazon Video, Shudder, YouTube, Vudu, and iTunes, on November 1, 2016. A sequel, Hell House LLC II: The Abaddon Hotel, was released on September 20, 2018. A second sequel, Hell House LLC III: Lake of Fire was released in September 2019, and a spinoff, Hell House LLC Origins: The Carmichael Manor was released in October 2023.

  • Found Footage Horror:

    "Hell House LLC" is a quintessential found footage horror film, presenting its story through the perspective of characters filming a documentary about a haunted attraction. The film utilizes handheld cameras, security camera footage, and other documentary-style techniques to immerse viewers in the terrifying events unfolding within the haunted house attraction.
