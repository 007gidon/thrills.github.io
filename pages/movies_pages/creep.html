Creep

Creep is a 2014 American found footage psychological horror film directed by Patrick Brice, his directorial debut, from a story by Brice and Mark Duplass, who both star in the film. Filmed as found footage, Brice portrays a videographer assigned to record an eccentric client, played by Duplass. Creep was inspired by Brice's experiences on Craigslist and the movies My Dinner with Andre, Misery, and Fatal Attraction. Brice and Duplass refined the film's story during filming, which resulted in multiple versions of each scene and several alternate end scenarios. The film premiered on March 8, 2014, at South by Southwest, and was released on video on demand on June 23, 2015, by The Orchard prior to an international release via Netflix on July 14, 2015. It received positive reviews from critics. A sequel was released in 2017, also directed by Brice and starring Duplass, with a third film planned for a future release.

  • Found Footage Horror:

    Like many films in the found footage genre, "Creep" presents its story through the lens of a handheld camera operated by one of the characters. This approach adds a sense of immediacy and realism to the narrative, drawing viewers into the unsettling events unfolding on screen.

  • Psychological Horror:

    At its core, "Creep" is a psychological horror film that focuses on the relationship between the two main characters, Josef and Aaron. The film explores themes of trust, manipulation, and the blurred lines between reality and fiction as Josef's behavior becomes increasingly erratic and disturbing.
