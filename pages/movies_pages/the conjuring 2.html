The Conjuring 2

The Conjuring 2 (known in the United Kingdom as The Conjuring 2: The Enfield Case) is a 2016 American supernatural horror film, directed by James Wan. The screenplay is by Chad Hayes, Carey W. Hayes, Wan, and David Leslie Johnson. It is the sequel to 2013's The Conjuring, the second installment in The Conjuring series, and the third installment in The Conjuring Universe franchise. Patrick Wilson and Vera Farmiga reprise their roles as paranormal investigators and authors Ed and Lorraine Warren from the first film. The film follows the Warrens as they travel to England to assist the Hodgson family, who are experiencing poltergeist activity at their Enfield council house in 1977 which later became referred to as the Enfield poltergeist. The Conjuring 2 had its world premiere at TCL Chinese Theatre on June 7, 2016, and was theatrically released in the United States on June 10, 2016, by Warner Bros. Pictures and New Line Cinema. The film received generally positive reviews from critics and grossed over $320 million worldwide. A spin-off prequel, The Nun, was released in September 2018, and a sequel, The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It, was released in June 2021.

"The Conjuring 2" embodies a unique blend of two distinct horror subgenres:

  • Possession Horror:

    A significant aspect of the film involves the possession of one of the Hodgson family members, Janet, by a malevolent entity. The possession theme adds a chilling dimension to the horror, as Janet's struggle against the entity becomes increasingly intense and terrifying.

  • Haunted House:

    The film revolves around the Hodgson family, who experience terrifying supernatural occurrences in their home. Much of the horror stems from the haunted house setting, with the family tormented by malevolent spirits and poltergeist activity.
