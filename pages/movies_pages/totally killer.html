Totally Killer

Totally Killer is a 2023 American slasher comedy film directed by Nahnatchka Khan from a screenplay by David Matalon, Sasha Perl-Raver, and Jen D'Angelo, and a screen story by Matalon and Perl-Raver. Produced by Jason Blum, under his Blumhouse Television banner, and Adam Hendricks and Greg Gilreath under their Divide/Conquer banner, it stars Kiernan Shipka, Olivia Holt, and Julie Bowen. The story follows Jamie (Shipka) who, after her mother is murdered by the same Sweet 16 Killer who went on a murder spree 35 years earlier, travels back in time to 1987, where she pairs up with her mother to catch the killer during his original spree and return to her timeline before she is trapped in the past forever. Totally Killer premiered at Fantastic Fest on September 28, 2023, and was released on Amazon Prime Video on October 6, 2023.

  • Slasher Horror:

    "Totally Killer" (2023) revitalizes the slasher subgenre with its inventive blend of comedy, time-travel sci-fi, and horror elements. Directed by Nahnatchka Khan and starring Kiernan Shipka, the film follows Jamie Hughes as she accidentally travels back to 1987 and tries to prevent a series of murders known as the Sweet Sixteen Killings. With its witty dialogue, engaging performances, and playful homage to 1980s culture, "Totally Killer" offers a fresh and entertaining twist on the classic slasher formula.

  • Comedy Horror:

    "Totally Killer" (2023) incorporates comedy elements to add levity and humor to its narrative. Directed by Nahnatchka Khan, the film infuses witty dialogue, amusing one-liners, and situational comedy into its plot. The comedic moments often stem from Jamie Hughes' fish-out-of-water experiences as she navigates the culture shock of being transported from 2023 to 1987. Additionally, the film cleverly juxtaposes modern sensibilities with 1980s norms, leading to humorous observations and interactions between characters. Despite its dark subject matter, "Totally Killer" maintains a lighthearted tone through its comedy, providing audiences with an entertaining and enjoyable viewing experience.
