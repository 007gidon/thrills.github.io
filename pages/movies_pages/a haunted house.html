A Haunted House

"A Haunted House" is a 2013 American found footage horror-comedy film directed by Michael Tiddes and written by Marlon Wayans and Rick Alvarez. The film is a parody of the "found footage" genre popularized by films like "Paranormal Activity." It follows the story of Malcolm and Kisha, a couple who move into a new home, only to discover that it is haunted by supernatural forces. The film combines elements of horror and comedy to create a blend of scares and laughs. As Malcolm and Kisha attempt to deal with the increasingly bizarre and terrifying occurrences in their home, they enlist the help of various experts and paranormal investigators to rid themselves of the malevolent spirits. "A Haunted House" features over-the-top humor, slapstick comedy, and exaggerated performances, all of which contribute to its comedic appeal. The film also includes numerous references and parodies of popular horror movies, adding an additional layer of humor for fans of the genre. While "A Haunted House" received mixed reviews from critics, it found success at the box office and has since gained a cult following as a guilty pleasure for fans of horror-comedy films. It spawned a sequel, "A Haunted House 2," which was released in 2014. Overall, "A Haunted House" is a lighthearted and entertaining spoof that offers a humorous take on the conventions of the horror genre.

"A Haunted House" embodies a unique blend of three distinct horror subgenres:

  • Found Footage Parody:

    The film satirizes the found footage subgenre, which typically presents the story through footage purportedly filmed by the characters themselves. "A Haunted House" mocks the conventions of found footage horror, including shaky camerawork, jump scares, and supernatural occurrences captured on camera.

  • Horror Comedy:

    Like many of Marlon Wayans' films, "A Haunted House" blends elements of horror with comedy. The movie relies on exaggerated situations, slapstick humor, and over-the-top performances to elicit laughs from the audience.
