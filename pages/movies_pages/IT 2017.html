IT 2017

"It" (2017) is a supernatural horror film directed by Andy Muschietti, based on the 1986 novel of the same name by Stephen King. Here's an overview of the movie: Set in the small town of Derry, Maine, during the late 1980s, "It" follows a group of outcast kids known as the Losers' Club who band together to confront a malevolent entity that preys on children and takes the form of a sinister clown named Pennywise. As the Losers' Club faces off against Pennywise (played by Bill Skarsgård) and confronts their own fears and traumas, they realize that they must stick together to defeat the ancient evil terrorizing their town. Along the way, they uncover the truth about Pennywise's origins and the sinister forces at work in Derry. "It" blends elements of horror, coming-of-age drama, and supernatural thriller, capturing both the terror of confronting a shape-shifting monster and the poignant journey of friendship and resilience. With its atmospheric setting, standout performances, and iconic villain, "It" became a critical and commercial success, revitalizing interest in Stephen King's work and paving the way for a highly anticipated sequel.

"IT 2017" embodies a unique blend of three distinct horror subgenres:

  • Supernatural Horror:

    The film features supernatural elements, including the malevolent entity Pennywise, who terrorizes the town of Derry and preys on children's fears.

  • Coming-of-Age Horror:

    The story revolves around a group of children, known as the Losers' Club, who confront both the horrors of Pennywise and the challenges of growing up.

  • Creature Feature Horror:

    Pennywise, the antagonist of the film, is a shape-shifting entity that often takes on the form of a sinister clown, making it a central creature in the narrative.
