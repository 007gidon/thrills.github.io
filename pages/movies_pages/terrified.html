Terrified

"Terrified" (original title: "Aterrados") is a 2017 Argentine supernatural horror film directed by Demián Rugna. "Terrified" follows a series of inexplicable and terrifying events that plague a suburban neighborhood in Buenos Aires, Argentina. When residents begin experiencing paranormal phenomena and encounters with malevolent entities, they turn to a team of investigators for help in unraveling the mystery. As the investigators delve deeper into the occurrences, they uncover a dark and sinister force that threatens the entire community. The film weaves together multiple storylines, each focusing on different characters who are affected by the supernatural events unfolding around them. With its chilling atmosphere, intense scares, and inventive use of practical effects, "Terrified" delivers a truly unsettling and unforgettable horror experience. The film explores themes of fear, paranoia, and the unknown, leaving viewers on the edge of their seats as they witness the escalating terror faced by the characters. "Terrified" has garnered widespread acclaim from horror fans and critics alike for its inventive approach to the genre and its ability to deliver genuine scares. Demián Rugna's direction, coupled with strong performances and effective cinematography, cements the film as a standout in contemporary horror cinema.

"Terrified" embodies a unique blend of two distinct horror subgenres:

  • Supernatural Horror:

    "Terrified" revolves around supernatural phenomena occurring in a neighborhood, including strange and terrifying occurrences that defy logical explanation. The film explores the fear and dread induced by paranormal activity and the unknown.

  • Haunting Horror:

    The film features elements of haunting horror, with residents of a neighborhood experiencing terrifying encounters with malevolent entities within their own homes. These hauntings disrupt their sense of safety and sanity, leading to escalating levels of terror.
