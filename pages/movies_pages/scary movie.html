Scary Movie

"Scary Movie" is a 2000 American comedy horror film directed by Keenen Ivory Wayans. It parodies various horror films, primarily those in the "slasher" subgenre, including "Scream," "I Know What You Did Last Summer," and "The Blair Witch Project." The film follows a group of teenagers who are stalked and terrorized by a masked killer known as "Ghostface." As they try to uncover the identity of the killer and survive the night, they encounter a series of absurd and over-the-top situations, often poking fun at common horror movie tropes and clichés. "Scary Movie" is known for its irreverent humor, slapstick comedy, and rapid-fire jokes. It features a star-studded cast, including Anna Faris, Shawn Wayans, Marlon Wayans, and Regina Hall, who deliver hilarious performances as they navigate the chaotic and nonsensical world of horror movie parodies. The film was a commercial success and spawned several sequels, each continuing the tradition of spoofing popular horror films while adding its own comedic twist. Despite its critical reception, "Scary Movie" remains a beloved cult classic among fans of horror and comedy alike.

"Scary Movie" embodies a unique blend of three distinct horror subgenres:

  • Parody:

    "Scary Movie" is a parody film, specifically spoofing the conventions and tropes of the horror genre. It satirizes various horror films, including "Scream," "I Know What You Did Last Summer," and "The Blair Witch Project," among others.

  • Comedy Horror:

    The film blends horror elements with comedic elements, resulting in a humorous take on typical horror movie scenarios. It includes slapstick humor, visual gags, and over-the-top comedic performances.

  • Slasher Parody:

    Given its primary inspiration from the "Scream" franchise, "Scary Movie" parodies the slasher subgenre, which typically features masked killers stalking and murdering victims. It mocks the clichés and conventions of slasher films while also incorporating elements of gore and violence for comedic effect.
