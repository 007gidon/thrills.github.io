Wrong Turn

"Wrong Turn" is a 2003 horror film directed by Rob Schmidt. Here's an overview of the movie: "Wrong Turn" follows a group of six people who become stranded in the remote forests of West Virginia after their car breaks down. As they search for help, they stumble upon an isolated cabin inhabited by a family of deformed cannibals who have been living in the woods for generations. As the group tries to escape, they find themselves hunted by the cannibalistic family, who will stop at nothing to protect their territory and their gruesome way of life. The survivors must rely on their wits and resourcefulness to outsmart their pursuers and make it out of the forest alive. The film blends elements of survival horror and slasher tropes, delivering tense and suspenseful sequences as the characters navigate the treacherous terrain and face off against their monstrous adversaries. With its atmospheric setting, gruesome practical effects, and intense chase scenes, "Wrong Turn" offers a thrilling and visceral horror experience. "Wrong Turn" spawned several sequels and became a cult classic in the horror genre, known for its memorable villains, brutal kills, and relentless pacing. Fans of backwoods horror and survival thrillers will appreciate its blend of tension, gore, and action.
