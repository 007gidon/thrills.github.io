The Ritual

"The Ritual" is a 2017 British horror film directed by David Bruckner, based on the novel of the same name by Adam Nevill. The film stars Rafe Spall, Arsher Ali, Robert James-Collier, and Sam Troughton as four friends who embark on a hiking trip in the Scandinavian wilderness to honor their deceased friend. As they journey through the remote forests of Sweden, the group encounters eerie and unsettling phenomena. When they take a shortcut through the woods, they become lost and find themselves stalked by a malevolent presence. As tensions rise and their sanity begins to unravel, they realize they are being hunted by an ancient and sinister entity. "The Ritual" blends elements of psychological horror, survival thriller, and folk horror to create a gripping and atmospheric experience. The film's remote setting and sense of isolation add to the growing sense of dread, while its exploration of guilt, trauma, and the supernatural adds depth to the narrative. Bruckner's direction emphasizes suspense and tension, building towards a chilling climax that delivers both scares and emotional resonance. The film's creature design and practical effects contribute to its unsettling atmosphere, keeping audiences on the edge of their seats until the very end.

"The Ritual" embodies a unique blend of three distinct horror subgenres:

  • Survival Horror:

    "The Ritual" is primarily a survival horror film, as the characters must contend with the harsh wilderness and the dangers lurking within it. As they struggle to find their way out of the forest, they face physical challenges, psychological trauma, and the threat of a supernatural entity.

  • Folk Horror:

    The film draws heavily from the subgenre of folk horror, incorporating elements of ancient rituals, pagan beliefs, and rural superstitions. The setting in the Scandinavian wilderness evokes a sense of primal terror and ancestral dread, rooted in the folklore and traditions of the region.
