The Sadness

"The Sadness" is a 2021 Taiwanese horror film directed by Rob Jabbaz. Set in Taiwan, the film follows a young couple, Kat and Jim, as they navigate a city plunged into chaos by an unprecedented viral outbreak. This outbreak causes infected individuals to succumb to violent and uncontrollable impulses, leading to widespread chaos and devastation. The movie explores themes of societal collapse, human nature, and the fragility of civilization in the face of catastrophe. As Kat and Jim struggle to survive amidst the carnage, they encounter other survivors who have been pushed to their limits by the horrors of the outbreak. Together, they must confront not only the physical dangers posed by the infected but also the psychological toll of witnessing humanity's darkest impulses unleashed. "The Sadness" is renowned for its intense and unflinching portrayal of violence, as well as its unapologetic exploration of the darkest aspects of human behavior. The film's relentless pace and visceral imagery have earned it a reputation as a harrowing and unforgettable viewing experience, drawing comparisons to other notable entries in the horror genre.

"The Sadness" embodies a unique blend of three distinct horror subgenres:

  • Zombie Horror:

    "The Sadness" features a viral outbreak that turns infected individuals into violent and aggressive killers. While not traditional zombies, the infected behave similarly, attacking and spreading the virus to others. This aligns the film with the zombie horror subgenre.

  • Splatter Horror:

    "The Sadness" employs the Splatter subgenre through its vivid and explicit portrayal of violence, gore, and bodily mutilation. These graphic scenes contribute to the film's intense and shocking atmosphere, leaving a lasting impact on viewers with their visceral nature.

  • Body Horror:

    The film includes graphic and grotesque scenes of bodily transformation and mutilation as a result of the viral outbreak. These elements contribute to the body horror subgenre, which focuses on the physical deterioration and transformation of the human body.
